## Learning rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | Score and reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism and units | Missing or materially wrong | Plausible with a stated gap | Correct reasoning, units and changed-input explanation | |
| Evidence and limits | Unsupported or fabricated result | Relevant but incomplete evidence | Traceable evidence with scope, positive control and uncertainty | |
| Service and recovery | No usable acceptance/recovery reasoning | Partial criteria or recovery | Appropriate service test, failure handling and verified restoration | |
| Communication and ownership | Another person cannot continue | Main issue clear, some missing detail | Clear decision, ownership, handover and next action | |

For a paper milestone, assess a proposed test and recovery plan as a plan; do not award it credit for execution. For practical milestones, require actual observed results. Aim for 6/8 with no zero, then correct material technical and recovery errors. The rubric is for learning and has not been validated as a personnel-selection instrument.
